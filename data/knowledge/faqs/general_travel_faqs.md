# General Travel FAQs

**How far in advance should I book flights for the best price?**
For international trips, booking six to eight weeks ahead generally offers a good balance between price and flight availability. Booking too early, more than four months out, or too late, under two weeks, both tend to increase prices for most routes.

**Is travel insurance worth it for a short trip?**
Even for trips of a few days, travel insurance is generally recommended if the trip involves any nonrefundable bookings, since medical emergencies or trip cancellations can cost far more than the insurance premium itself.

**How much cash should I carry versus using cards?**
In most major cities, cards are widely accepted and carrying large amounts of cash is unnecessary and increases theft risk. Keeping a small amount of local currency for markets, tips, or small vendors that do not accept cards is still a good idea.

**What is the best way to avoid jet lag?**
Adjusting sleep schedule gradually a few days before departure, staying hydrated during the flight, and getting sunlight exposure at the destination as soon as possible after arrival are the most effective, low-cost strategies for reducing jet lag.

**Should I exchange currency before I leave or after arrival?**
Exchanging a small amount before departure covers immediate needs like taxis, but airport exchange counters typically offer poor rates. Withdrawing cash from an ATM after arrival, using a card with low foreign transaction fees, usually gives a better exchange rate than either option.

**How do I pack light for a two-week trip?**
Planning outfits around a small number of interchangeable pieces, choosing quick-dry fabrics that can be washed and worn again, and packing for laundry access rather than a fresh outfit every day are the most reliable ways to avoid overpacking.
